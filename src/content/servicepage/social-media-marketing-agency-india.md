I've been managing social media for Indian businesses since 2012. Back then, having any social presence was enough. Today? If your social media isn't driving qualified leads and sales, you're wasting resources.

That's where most social media agencies in India fail. They're focused on engagement and followers. We're focused on pipeline generation and revenue attribution.

### The Problem We Fix Every Day:
Businesses posting daily with zero ROI. Beautiful feeds with terrible conversion rates. Agencies celebrating viral posts while clients wonder where the sales are. It's frustrating to watch businesses invest time and money in social media that doesn't contribute to their bottom line.

### Our Three-Phase Process That Actually Works:

**Phase 1: Revenue-Aligned Social Audit**
We don't start with content calendars. We start with your business objectives. What's your customer acquisition cost target? What's your ideal customer profile? What social proof matters to your buyers? We audit your current social media against revenue metrics, not just engagement metrics. This phase alone has helped Surat jewelry brands identify ₹25 lakh/year in missed social commerce opportunities.

**Phase 2: Platform-Specific Strategy Build**
Now we build strategies. But not one-size-fits-all. Instagram for visual storytelling and direct sales. LinkedIn for authority building and B2B leads. YouTube for educational content and brand authority. WhatsApp for customer service and conversions. We've helped Bangalore SaaS companies generate 200+ qualified leads/month from LinkedIn and Delhi fashion brands achieve 20% of revenue from Instagram Shopping.

**Phase 3: Continuous Revenue Optimization**
Social media isn't set-and-forget. We analyze performance data weekly. We test new content formats monthly. We optimize posting times based on audience behavior. We make data-driven decisions that increase your social media ROI quarter after quarter.

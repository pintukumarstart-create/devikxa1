## About Our PPC Approach: It's About Profitability, Not Just Traffic

I've been managing PPC campaigns for Indian businesses since 2010. Back then, getting clicks was enough. Today? If your PPC isn't delivering profitable customers, you're burning cash.

That's where most PPC services in India fail. They're focused on clicks and impressions. We're focused on profit margins and customer lifetime value.

### The Problem We Fix Every Day:
Businesses spending lakhs on ads with negative ROI. Beautiful campaigns with terrible conversion rates. Agencies optimizing for "engagement" while clients lose money. It's painful to watch businesses waste advertising budgets that could actually grow their revenue.

### Our Three-Phase Process That Actually Works:

**Phase 1: Profitability-Focused Audit**
We don't start with keywords. We start with your numbers. What's your average order value? What's your acceptable customer acquisition cost? What's a customer worth over 12 months? We audit your current PPC against profitability metrics, not just performance metrics. This phase alone has helped Ahmedabad manufacturers identify 40% wasted ad spend.

**Phase 2: Conversion-Centric Campaign Build**
Now we build campaigns. But not for traffic - for conversions. We create ad copy that speaks to buying intent. We structure accounts for maximum relevance. We optimize landing pages for mobile conversions. We've helped Delhi retailers achieve 8:1 ROAS and Mumbai e-commerce brands reduce CAC by 60%.

**Phase 3: Continuous Profit Optimization**
PPC isn't set-and-forget. We optimize daily based on performance data. We test new ad variations weekly. We adjust bids hourly based on conversion likelihood. We make data-driven decisions that increase your profitability week after week.

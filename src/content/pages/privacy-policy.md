## Information We Collect

## How We Use Your Information

- We cannot use other than your information to answer your questions, and to notify you the projects or services by email.
- To provide web design, development, and digital marketing services, we need to use customer data.
- To make the users' experience, the website or services, and the user experience better, utilization of the users' data is a must.
- We perform the use of information for legal and regulatory purposes only.

---

## Data Security & Privacy

- We put in place technical and organizational security that is adequate for our purposes.
- Access to personal data is limited to people who are authorized.
- We never sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing use.

You have the option of turning off or blocking certain cookies via your browser settings.

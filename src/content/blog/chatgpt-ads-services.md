## What Does a ChatGPT Ads Service Actually Do?

A true **chatgpt ads service** does not just treat AI like traditional search. ChatGPT Ads are not standard text ads. You aren't bidding on exact match keywords, and your ads aren't crammed into a busy, infinite-scroll feed.

When a user on the ChatGPT Free or Go tier asks a question with commercial intent, OpenAI’s system evaluates the context of the conversation. If your brand is a semantic match, a dedicated *chat_card* appears below the AI's response.

## The Paradigm Shift: Why You Need a Specialized ChatGPT Ads Service

If a general marketing agency tells you they will "bid on your top keywords in ChatGPT," run the other way. You need a specialized **chatgpt ads service** that understands OpenAI's unique ecosystem.

OpenAI doesn't use lexical keyword matching. Instead, the platform relies on **Context Hints**. Context hints are natural-language descriptions placed at the ad-group level that tell ChatGPT which specific conversational journeys fit your offer. 

Here is how targeting with our **chatgpt ads service** differs from traditional search management:

| Advertising Element | Google Search Ads | ChatGPT Ads | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Matching Logic** | Lexical triggers (exact, phrase, broad match) | Semantic relevance to the user's overall conversational intent | 
| **Advertiser Input** | Lists of isolated keywords (e.g., "CRM software") | Natural language Context Hints at the ad-group level | 
| **User Mindset** | Transactional or quick information retrieval | Deep research, active problem-solving, multi-step comparison | 
| **Ad Placement** | Blended into a crowded SERP | A standalone visual *chat_card* below the AI's response |
